# ASSOCADS Brand Guide

**Version 1.2 · November 2026 · For the website, social channels, presentations, documents and event material**

This guide fixes the colours, fonts and usage rules for the Association for AI and Data Science so that every channel looks like the same organisation. It is also the design source of truth for the website build: copy it into the website repository as `docs/BRAND_GUIDE.md`.

Every colour pairing below has been checked against WCAG 2.2 contrast requirements. The ratios are stated so that nobody has to guess.

---

## 1. Brand foundations

### 1.1 Name

| Use                                   | Form                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Short name                            | **ASSOCADS** (always capitals, one word, no dots, no hyphen)                                  |
| Full name                             | **Association for AI and Data Science**                                                       |
| First mention in any document or page | Association for AI and Data Science (ASSOCADS)                                                |
| Legal description, until registration | "a public charitable trust (registration in progress)"                                        |
| Legal description, after registration | "a registered public charitable trust" plus the registration number                           |
| Never                                 | Assocads, AssocADS, A.S.S.O.C.A.D.S., "the ASSOCADS Trust Foundation" or any invented variant |

### 1.2 Positioning

- **What it is, in one sentence:** ASSOCADS is a not-for-profit professional association that connects students, professionals, academia, industry, startups and government to advance AI and Data Science in India, beginning in West Bengal.
- **Primary line:** _Connecting talent, knowledge, innovation and opportunity._ (taken from the trust deed)
- **Shorter alternatives for tight spaces:** _India's community for AI and Data Science._ / _Where AI talent meets opportunity._
- **Long-term ambition, used sparingly:** to become an apex professional body for AI and Data Science.

### 1.3 Personality

Credible, open, precise, optimistic. The reference is a respected professional institute, not a startup and not a coaching centre.

### 1.4 Voice

| Do                                                              | Don't                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Plain, direct sentences. "Join a workshop. Meet a mentor."      | Hype: "revolutionary", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "game-changing" |
| Specific and verifiable: dates, places, named people            | Numbers that have not happened yet shown as achievements              |
| "We plan to", "We aim to" for anything in the future            | "We have trained 5,000 members" before it is true                     |
| Indian English spelling: organisation, programme, centre        | Mixed US and UK spelling                                              |
| Sentence case for headings: "Become a member"                   | Title Case Or ALL CAPS Headings                                       |
| Explain a term the first time: "Special Interest Groups (SIGs)" | Unexplained acronyms                                                  |

### 2.4 Pairings that are not allowed

| Text                | Background     | Ratio     | Why                                                                     |
| ------------------- | -------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Marigold `#F2A31B`  | Paper or Cloud | 2.1:1     | Fails. Use Amber Deep for marigold-family text on light.                |
| White               | Marigold       | 2.1:1     | Fails. Text on Marigold is always Ink.                                  |
| Slate 400 `#9AA1B2` | Paper          | below 3:1 | Fails. Slate 400 is for dark backgrounds only.                          |
| Line `#D9DCE5`      | Paper          | 1.4:1     | Borders only. Never for text or for the only outline of an input field. |

### 2.5 Proportion

Aim for roughly **70% Paper and Cloud, 20% Ink, 8% Indigo, 2% Marigold** across any page, slide or poster. Indigo marks what can be clicked or what matters most. Marigold appears at most once per screen.

### 2.6 Data visualisation order

Charts use colours in this order: Indigo `#2F3BD9`, Teal `#0B7A6E`, Marigold `#F2A31B`, Slate 600 `#4A5165`, Indigo Bright `#8791FF`. Always label series directly so that colour is never the only way to tell them apart.

### 2.7 Dark theme pairings

The founders approved the dark theme pairings in this section and the tokens in 2.8 on 26 November 2026.

The website has a dark theme. By default it follows the visitor's own clock: dark from 7 pm to 7 am, light at other times. The footer lets the visitor choose "Time of day", "System" (the device's setting), "Light" or "Dark", and the header switch sets light or dark. The browser remembers the choice. It uses only the palette above: Ink becomes the page, Night the alternate surface, and text turns light. Ink sections keep their usual look, on Night. Forms and the brand kit stay light in the dark theme, because Error, Success and Teal have no pairing on dark backgrounds that passes for text.

## 4. Logo

The logo is a triangular letter "A" built from four indigo segments that meet around a single marigold point. It stands for separate communities (students, professionals, industry and government) converging on a shared centre.

> Status: this logo is awaiting adoption by the Board and has not yet been checked against registered trademarks. Complete both before printing anything permanent.

The master files are in the `ASSOCADS_Logo_Final` folder. Copy that folder into the website repository as `docs/logo/`. Always use these files. Never redraw, retype or screenshot the logo.

### 4.1 Layouts

| Layout                    | File name starts with                     | Use it for                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Horizontal                | `assocads-logo-horizontal-`               | The default: website header, letterhead, slides, email footers |
| Horizontal, no descriptor | `assocads-logo-horizontal-no-descriptor-` | Small sizes and tight spaces, below about 160px wide           |
| Stacked                   | `assocads-logo-stacked-`                  | Square spaces, posters, certificates, event backdrops          |
| Symbol                    | `assocads-symbol-`                        | Where the name already appears close by                        |
| App icon                  | `assocads-app-icon`                       | Social profile pictures, the favicon, app tiles                |

### 4.2 Colour versions

| Version          | Appearance                                            | Use on                                            |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `colour`         | Indigo symbol, marigold point, Ink lettering          | Paper and Cloud backgrounds. The first choice.    |
| `colour-on-dark` | Indigo Bright symbol, marigold point, white lettering | Ink and Night backgrounds                         |
| `indigo`         | Everything in Indigo                                  | One-colour print on light stock                   |
| `black`          | Everything in black                                   | Stamps, fax, engraving, black-and-white print     |
| `white`          | Everything in white                                   | Indigo backgrounds, photographs with a dark panel |

### 4.3 Construction

- **Symbol:** four rounded segments on a 60-degree grid with equal gaps between them, and one circle. The segments are ASSOCADS Indigo `#2F3BD9`; the circle is Marigold `#F2A31B`.
- **Wordmark:** ASSOCADS in Inter Tight ExtraBold (weight 800), capitals. This weight is reserved for the logo and is not used for headings.
- **Descriptor:** "Association for AI and Data Science" in Inter Medium, set to the same width as the wordmark.

### 4.4 Rules

- **Clear space:** keep a margin equal to the height of the letter "A" in the wordmark on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** horizontal logo 120px wide on screen and 30mm in print; without the descriptor, 80px and 20mm; symbol 20px. Below 20px use the app icon.
- **Backgrounds:** use only the pairings in section 4.2. On a photograph, place the logo on a solid Paper or Ink panel.
- **Never:** stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, change the colours, rearrange the symbol and wordmark, retype the name in another font, or use the full-colour version on Indigo.

### 5.1 Motion

Motion is quiet and purposeful, in the manner of Apple product pages.

- Durations: 150ms for hover and press, 300ms for menus and reveals, 600ms for large hero transitions.
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.
- Sections fade in and rise 16px once, as they enter the screen. Nothing loops, bounces or scrolls sideways on its own.
- Everything respects the "reduce motion" system setting.

## 6. Imagery and icons

- **Photography:** real ASSOCADS people and events, in natural light, with people doing things (teaching, presenting, building). No stock photos of robots, glowing brains, handshakes or blue binary rain.
- **Before real photos exist:** use abstract brand graphics instead of stock photos: a fine dot grid or thin connected-node lines in Indigo on Paper, or in Indigo Bright on Ink.
- **Portraits of trustees and speakers:** head and shoulders, plain light background, cropped square, shown in colour. Until a photo is supplied, show a Cloud-coloured tile with the person's initials in Inter Tight SemiBold, Slate 600.
- **Other organisations' logos:** shown only with that organisation's written permission, in a single neutral colour, at equal visual size.
- **Icons:** Lucide icon set, 1.5px stroke, in Ink or Indigo. No emoji in the interface.
- **AI-generated images:** permitted only for abstract backgrounds, never for people, events or anything that could be mistaken for a real photograph.

## 9. Quick checklist before anything is published

1. Is the name written ASSOCADS, with the full name on first mention?
2. Are only Inter Tight, Inter and JetBrains Mono used, and is the logo taken from the master files?
3. Is every colour from the palette, and does every text pairing appear in the approved table?
4. Is there one Marigold accent at most?
5. Are all facts true today, and is anything in the future worded as a plan?
6. Does the legal status line match the current registration status?
7. Has written permission been received for every third-party logo and every photograph of a person?
