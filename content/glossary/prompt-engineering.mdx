## In plain English

A prompt is the request you give a generative AI model, usually in ordinary language. It can contain a question, an instruction, background information, examples of what you want, or some text for the model to continue.

Prompt engineering is the craft of shaping that request well and improving it step by step. Useful techniques include stating the task clearly, giving the model the context it needs, setting the tone or format through system instructions, and including a few worked examples of the desired output. The core of the practice is iteration: try a prompt, check the response, adjust and try again.

## An example

A district office wants help drafting plain-language summaries of new government circulars in English. A first attempt, "Summarise this", gives uneven results. The staff revise the prompt: they explain who the readers are, ask for five short points in simple language, tell the model to say when something is unclear rather than guess, and include one good sample summary. Each output is still checked by an officer before it is sent out.

## Why it matters

The same model can give weak or strong results depending on how it is asked. Prompt engineering is a practical skill for students and professionals using generative AI for writing, coding, analysis or building applications.

It has limits. A well-written prompt cannot guarantee accurate output, and models can still produce confident errors. Prompts are best treated as one part of a wider system that includes testing, human review and care with confidential or personal information.
