The trust deed provides for working with government at every level, and for advising public institutions.

## What we plan to offer

- Input on policies, standards and frameworks for AI and data science, under [policy and advisory](/programmes/policy-and-advisory).
- Training and capacity building for public servants.
- Guidance on responsible AI, under [responsible AI](/programmes/responsible-ai).
- AI-for-social-good projects in areas such as healthcare, agriculture, education and governance, under [recognition and AI for social good](/programmes/awards-and-ai-for-good).
