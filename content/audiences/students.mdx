ASSOCADS is open to everyone, without discrimination, and students are one of the groups it was set up to serve.

## What we plan to offer

- Workshops, bootcamps, hackathons and meetups, under the [training and certification](/programmes/training-and-certification) and [events and community](/programmes/events-and-community) programmes.
- Mentoring, career guidance, and routes into internships and apprenticeships.
- Guides on careers in data science and AI, each reviewed by a named trustee.

## Can students join?

Ordinary membership is open to individuals aged eighteen or over. The trust deed allows a separate student category, but it must first be created by bye-laws adopted after registration. Until then, you can register your interest and we will tell you when membership opens.
