  - question: Can I join ASSOCADS as a student?
    answer: Yes. Student membership is for anyone enrolled in an undergraduate, postgraduate, PhD or diploma course, with a valid ID.   - question: Will there be anything for students under eighteen?
    answer: Student membership is for students in undergraduate, postgraduate, PhD or diploma courses. Whether some events will be open to school students will be stated on each event page.
  - question: How much does student membership cost?
    answer: ₹600 a year, or ₹300 through a member institution. You can also join for the length of your course at 15% off, and after you graduate your first year as an Early-career Professional is half price. Access seats are free or half price for students from economically weaker backgrounds, rural and Tier-3 institutions, and first-generation graduates.
