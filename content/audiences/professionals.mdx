faqs:
  - question: Is ASSOCADS only for people in industry?
    answer: No. It is for practitioners, faculty and researchers alike, and it aims to connect academia, industry, startups and government.
  - question: Which professional membership is right for me?
    answer: It depends on your experience. Early-career is for under five years and costs ₹1,500 a year, Mid-career is for five to twelve years and costs ₹3,000, and Senior or Leader is for over twelve years, or a CXO, Director or Principal, and costs ₹6,000. Freelancers and consultants can join as Independent members, and NGO staff, journalists and public servants as Public-interest members.
  - question: Can members vote or help run ASSOCADS?
    answer: No. Membership gives access to programmes and benefits, but no ownership, no share of any surplus and no seat on the Board of Trustees. Senior members may be invited to advisory panels.
  - question: What are Special Interest Groups?
    answer: Groups organised by industry or domain. The trust deed allows the Board to set them up, and they can offer training and advice in their field. None has been formed yet.
