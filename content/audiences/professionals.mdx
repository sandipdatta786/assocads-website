This page is for people who already work with AI, data science and analytics, in industry, in teaching or in research.

## What we plan to offer

- Advanced training, certification and faculty development, under the [training and certification](/programmes/training-and-certification) programme.
- Ways to publish and collaborate on research, open-source work and case studies, under [research and publications](/programmes/research-and-publications).
- Special Interest Groups by industry or domain, which the Board may set up under the trust deed.
- Chances to mentor students and early-career professionals.

## How to join

Ordinary and Life membership are open to individuals. Fees have not been fixed yet. You can register your interest now.
