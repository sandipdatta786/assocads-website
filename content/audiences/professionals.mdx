faqs:
  - question: Is ASSOCADS only for people in industry?
    answer: No. It is for practitioners, faculty and researchers alike, and it aims to connect academia, industry, startups and government.
  - question: What is the difference between Ordinary and Life membership?
    answer: Ordinary members pay an annual subscription. Life members pay a one-time amount and are recommended by two trustees. The Board will fix both amounts after registration.
  - question: Can members vote or help run ASSOCADS?
    answer: Under the trust deed, membership gives no right to manage the trust or to vote, unless bye-laws later provide otherwise. Members can take part in programmes, Special Interest Groups, events and publications.
  - question: What are Special Interest Groups?
    answer: Groups organised by industry or domain. The trust deed allows the Board to set them up, and they can offer training and advice in their field. None has been formed yet.
