Companies, startups and industry bodies are part of the ecosystem the trust deed sets out to build.

## What we plan to offer

- Links to students and graduates, and help with workforce readiness, under [industry and academia collaboration](/programmes/industry-and-academia).
- Training and advisory services for specific industries, directly or through Special Interest Groups.
- Research partnerships with academic groups.
- Support for founders through mentoring, incubation and investor engagement, under [startups and innovation](/programmes/startups-and-innovation).
- Sponsorship of events and programmes.

## How to work with us

Organisations can join as Associate Members once fees are fixed, or talk to us about a partnership. A partner is named on this site only after an agreement and written permission are in place.
