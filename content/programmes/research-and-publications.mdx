## What the trust deed provides for

This programme is based on the following objects of the ASSOCADS trust deed, written here in plain English.

- **Object 3.** Establish and support centres, labs, academies, libraries, knowledge repositories, innovation hubs, incubation centres and Centres of Excellence.
- **Object 5.** Undertake and publish research, journals, case studies, benchmark datasets, technical reports and white papers.
- **Object 11.** Promote collaborative, open-source and community-driven initiatives.
- **Object 15.** Create, protect and disseminate intellectual property.
