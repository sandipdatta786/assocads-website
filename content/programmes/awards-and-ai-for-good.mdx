## What the trust deed provides for

This programme is based on the following objects of the ASSOCADS trust deed, written here in plain English.

- **Object 8.** Recognise excellence through awards, fellowships, certifications and accreditations.
- **Object 12.** Design and support AI-for-Social-Good initiatives in healthcare, agriculture, education, governance, sustainability, climate resilience, public welfare, accessibility, rural development and social inclusion.

## Where it stands

This programme is planned. Activity has not started yet. When it does, this page will say so and list what is happening.
