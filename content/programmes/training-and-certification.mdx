## What the trust deed provides for

This programme is based on the following objects of the ASSOCADS trust deed, written here in plain English.

- **Object 2.** Run educational and capacity-building activities: conferences, seminars, workshops, bootcamps, hackathons, meetups, training, certification, faculty development, mentoring, career guidance, internships and apprenticeships.
- **Object 14.** Provide training and advisory services across industry domains, directly or through Special Interest Groups.
