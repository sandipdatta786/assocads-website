## Our own commitment

Responsible AI is not only something ASSOCADS promotes. It is one of the principles that bind the trust itself under its deed.

We intend to hold our own work to the same standard we promote. The guides we publish, the events we run and any AI tools we use or build should be responsible, ethical, transparent and trustworthy. The deed also binds the trust to data protection, academic integrity and conflict-of-interest rules. Read more about [how the trust is run](/about/governance).
