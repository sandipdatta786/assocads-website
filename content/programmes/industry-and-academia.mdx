## What the trust deed provides for

This programme is based on the following objects of the ASSOCADS trust deed, written here in plain English.

- **Object 4.** Foster collaboration between academia, industry, government, civil society and international institutions.
- **Object 9.** Collaborate with national and international organisations and take part in global initiatives.
