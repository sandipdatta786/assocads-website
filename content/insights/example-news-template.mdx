The first paragraph says the most important thing: what happened, when, and who it is for. Many readers stop here, so it should make sense on its own.

## What happens next

Use a heading when the article moves to a new topic. Most news items need one or two at most.

{/* A list: each line starts with a hyphen and a space. */}

- Use a list when there are three or more separate points.
- Keep each point to one line where you can.
- Write plans as plans, for example "we plan to" or "we aim to".

### A smaller heading

Use three hash signs for a heading inside a section. A link is written like this: read the [guides](/guides) for practical advice on careers and policy.

## How to take part

If readers can act on the news, say how in the last paragraph, and link to the right page, such as [register your interest](/membership/apply) or [contact us](/contact).
