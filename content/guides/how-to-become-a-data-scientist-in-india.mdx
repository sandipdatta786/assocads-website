Data science sits where statistics, programming and a working knowledge of a field meet. There is no single licensed route into it in India. People arrive from engineering, mathematics, statistics, economics and the sciences, and from roles such as software development or business analysis. This guide sets out the steps that most routes share.

## What does a data scientist do?

A data scientist uses data to answer questions and support decisions. The [US Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm) describes the work as finding relevant data, cleaning and analysing it, building and testing models (including machine learning models), visualising results and recommending actions. Its profile is written for the United States, but the tasks it lists are broadly the same in Indian organisations.

In practice, the job title covers a wide range. In one organisation a data scientist mainly writes SQL queries and builds dashboards; in another the same title means training and deploying machine learning models. Read job descriptions closely rather than relying on the title.

## What qualifications do you need?

A bachelor's degree in mathematics, statistics, computer science, engineering or a related subject is the usual minimum. Some employers, and most research roles, ask for a master's degree or a doctorate. Many people also move in from related work after building skills on the job.

You do not need a degree with "data science" in its name. What matters is that you can show the underlying skills: statistics, programming and the ability to explain what the data says.

## What skills should you learn?

Most data science roles draw on the same core set. Depth varies by role, but gaps in any of these tend to show quickly.

| Area | What to learn |
| --- | --- |
| Mathematics | Linear algebra, calculus, probability |
| Statistics | Descriptive statistics, sampling, hypothesis testing, regression |
| Programming | Python, including libraries for data handling and plotting |
| Data | SQL, data cleaning, working with messy or missing data |
| Machine learning | Supervised and unsupervised methods, model evaluation, overfitting |
| Communication | Writing clearly, charts, explaining results to people who are not specialists |

Knowledge of a field, such as finance, healthcare, agriculture or retail, is a strong advantage. It helps you ask useful questions and spot results that do not make sense.

## What are the steps to become a data scientist?

1. **Build the mathematical base.** Study linear algebra, calculus, probability and statistics, either in your degree or through courses alongside it.
2. **Learn to program.** Start with Python and SQL. Practise until you can load, clean, summarise and plot a dataset without copying code line by line.
3. **Learn core machine learning.** Understand how common methods work, how to split data for training and testing, and how to judge whether a model is any good.
4. **Do projects on real data.** Choose public datasets, ideally from Indian sources, and answer a clear question with each one. Write up what you did and what you found.
5. **Get practical experience.** An internship, research assistantship or entry-level analytics role teaches you how data work happens inside an organisation.
6. **Apply for roles that match your level.** Titles such as data analyst, junior data scientist, business analyst and analytics associate are common first steps.
7. **Keep learning.** Tools and methods change. Set aside regular time for courses, reading and new projects.

## Which free or public learning options exist in India?

Several public platforms offer AI and Data Science courses at low or no cost. Check each course page for current dates, fees and certificate rules before you enrol.

- **NPTEL.** [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in/) is an initiative of the IITs and IISc, funded by the Ministry of Education. Anyone can study its courses free. A certificate requires an optional proctored exam.
- **SWAYAM.** [SWAYAM](https://swayam.gov.in/about) is the Ministry of Education's course platform. Courses are free to learners, while the proctored exam for a certificate has a fee. Under a 2016 UGC regulation, universities may accept credits from approved SWAYAM courses, so ask your institution whether it does.
- **IIT Madras online BS degree.** IIT Madras offers an online [BS degree in Data Science and Applications](https://study.iitm.ac.in/ds/), with the option to exit at foundation, diploma or BSc level. Its [course list](https://study.iitm.ac.in/ds/academics.html) is a useful outline of what a full programme covers, even if you study elsewhere.
- **NIELIT.** The National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology runs short courses, including self-paced ones on its [NIELIT Virtual Academy](https://nva.nielit.gov.in/), where the course list included a certificate course in AI and machine learning using Python when we checked. NIELIT is a body under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.

Many commercial training providers and bootcamps also offer data science courses. This guide does not recommend or rank them. If you consider one, ask for a full syllabus, the total cost and what support is actually included, and compare it with the free options above.

## How do you show employers what you can do?

Employers hiring for a first role often have little to go on beyond your degree. Clear evidence of practical work helps.

- **A small portfolio.** Three or four well-documented projects are more useful than many unfinished ones. Each should state the question, the data, the method, the result and its limits.
- **Readable code.** Keep code in a public repository with a short explanation of how to run it.
- **Written explanations.** A short article or report explaining a project shows that you can communicate, which many data roles need.
- **Honest descriptions.** Describe only what you did yourself. Interviewers often ask detailed questions about projects listed on a CV.

## Where can you find internships and first jobs?

Internships are one of the most common ways into a first data role. The [AICTE National Internship Portal](https://internship.aicte-india.org/), run under the Ministry of Education, states that it is free for students and showed a category for AI, ML and data internships when we checked. Your college placement cell, faculty research projects and company career pages are other routes. Our guide on [how to find a data science or AI internship](/guides/how-to-find-a-data-science-or-ai-internship) covers this in more detail.

## How long does it take?

There is no fixed answer. A student taking a quantitative degree can build most of the core skills within the degree if they add programming and projects. A working professional moving from a related field may take longer, depending on the time available each week. Be wary of any course that promises a guaranteed job or a fixed timeline.

Pick the step you are on and do one concrete thing this week: enrol in a course, start a project or update your portfolio.
