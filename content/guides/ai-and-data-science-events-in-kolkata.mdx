In and around Kolkata, the main recurring events come from professional societies, academic institutes and volunteer community groups. Dates, venues and fees change every year. Treat every date here as a record of a past or announced edition, and check the organiser's own page before you plan to attend.

## What is CALCON?

CALCON is the conference of the IEEE Kolkata Section, a local section of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers. The [CALCON 2026 site](https://ewh.ieee.org/r10/calcutta/calcon2026/index.html) lists the ninth edition for 11 and 12 December 2026 in Kolkata, and shows past editions going back to 2005.

Its [topics of interest](https://ewh.ieee.org/r10/calcutta/calcon2026/topic.html) include a computer science track covering machine learning and deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and trustworthy and explainable AI. It is a research conference built around peer-reviewed papers, so check the registration page for the options open to non-authors.

## What is the ISI machine intelligence workshop?

The Machine Intelligence Unit of the Indian Statistical Institute in Kolkata runs an annual workshop with invited speakers on machine intelligence and its applications. The [2026 edition](https://www.isical.ac.in/~miu/MIU_Workshop_2026.html) was held on 11 March 2026 in the PJAB Auditorium at ISI Kolkata. The unit's page does not say who may attend, so contact the unit before planning to go.

## What is DevFest Kolkata?

DevFest is a series of developer conferences run by local Google Developer Groups around the world. [DevFest Kolkata 2025](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-kolkata-presents-devfest-kolkata-2025/), organised by GDG Kolkata, took place on 21 December 2025 in New Town. Its event page lists AI and Gemini among its topics, alongside Android, Firebase and Flutter.

GDG Kolkata and campus-based Google Developer Groups also hold smaller meetups and study sessions during the year. They are a practical way for students and early-career developers to meet people working with AI tools.

## Are there hackathons and student contests?

Yes. Hackathons and contests are common in student festivals, and a few focus on data science.

- **Kharagpur Data Science Hackathon.** IIT Kharagpur's Technology Students' Gymkhana lists the [Kharagpur Data Science Hackathon 2026](https://gymkhana.iitkgp.ac.in/events), held in January 2026 in association with Kshitij. Kharagpur is outside Kolkata but within West Bengal.
- **Kshitij.** The same page describes Kshitij as IIT Kharagpur's annual techno-management festival. Its 23rd edition ran from 16 to 18 January 2026 with more than 40 events.
- **ACM Kolkata Computer Science Project Contest.** The [2026 contest](https://kolkata.acm.org/UGproject/2026) invited teams of undergraduates in computer science and allied subjects from institutions in East and North-East India, nominated by their institutes. Finalists presented on 30 May 2026 at the University of Calcutta's Salt Lake campus. The page does not say whether the contest is held every year.

Other colleges in Kolkata run technology festivals with coding and AI events. We have not listed them because we could not confirm current dates on an official page.

## How do I keep track of new events?

Most events are announced only a few weeks ahead. A short routine helps you catch them.

1. **Follow the organisers directly.** Bookmark the official pages linked in this guide, and check them every month or two.
2. **Join a community group.** Membership of a group such as GDG Kolkata, or a student chapter of IEEE or ACM at your college, usually brings event announcements.
3. **Watch institute notice boards.** Universities and research institutes post workshops, talks and contests on their own websites.
4. **Look at public programmes.** State bodies sometimes run AI training or internships. Webel's Centre of Excellence on Data Science and Machine Learning, for example, [advertised internships](https://webel.in/Internship-programme-Data-Science-and-Machine-Learning-2025) in February 2025.

## How do I get the most from an event?

Choose events that match your level. A research conference such as CALCON suits people reading or writing papers, while a community meetup or hackathon suits those who want to build things and meet peers.

Before you go, read the programme and pick two or three sessions. Afterwards, write a short note on what you learned and follow up with one or two people you met. If you take part in a hackathon, keep the code and a short write-up: it becomes evidence of your skills.
