Many of the IT, consulting and professional services offices listed below are in Salt Lake Sector V and New Town, according to the companies' own location pages. Alongside them, a few public research bodies and state agencies work on AI and data. This guide lists organisations whose Kolkata presence we confirmed on their own websites on 23 November 2026.

Being on this list is not an endorsement, a ranking or a sign of any link with ASSOCADS. Office locations and teams change, so check each employer's careers page for current openings.

## Which IT and consulting companies have offices in Kolkata?

These companies are listed in alphabetical order. Each line says what the company does and where its own website confirms its Kolkata presence.

- **Accenture**: professional services and technology consulting. Its global locations page lists a Kolkata office at Candor Techspace in New Town.
- **Capgemini**: IT services and consulting. Its Kolkata page says it has been in the city since 2006 and that its Candor Tech Space office houses about 7,000 colleagues.
- **Cognizant**: IT services and consulting. It opened a facility at Infospace Tech Park, Kolkata, in November 2022, and said at the time that it had more than 18,300 associates in the city.
- **IBM**: technology and consulting. In November 2023, IBM Consulting listed Kolkata among its 12 Client Innovation Center locations in India.
- **Infosys**: IT services and consulting. It opened a development centre in Kolkata in December 2024 and said staff there would work on technologies including cloud and AI.
- **ITC Infotech**: technology services, a wholly owned subsidiary of ITC Limited. Its contact page lists offices at Virginia House on J. L. Nehru Road and at Green Centre in New Town.
- **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)**: IT services and consulting. In July 2026 it launched a Gemini Experience Center in Kolkata, built with Google Cloud, to show AI solutions for retail, consumer goods and travel businesses.
- **Wipro**: IT services and consulting. Its locations page lists three Kolkata Development Centres in Salt Lake Sector V.

## Which professional services firms work from Kolkata?

Large accounting and advisory firms also hire analysts and data specialists. Two with a confirmed Kolkata base are listed below.

- **EY**: assurance, tax and consulting services. Its India locations page lists a Kolkata office on Camac Street.
- **PwC**: assurance, tax and advisory services. PricewaterhouseCoopers Service Delivery Center (Kolkata) Private Limited, part of PwC's Acceleration Centres, has its registered office in Salt Lake Sector V.

## Which public bodies work on AI and data in Kolkata?

Public research bodies and state agencies offer a different kind of work: research projects, government systems and training. They usually advertise posts, fellowships and internships on their own websites.

- **C-DAC Kolkata**: a centre of the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing, which its page describes as an R&D organisation of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. Its listed work includes AI and machine learning based machine vision, agricultural data analytics, image processing and speech recognition.
- **Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata**: a teaching and research institute. Its Machine Intelligence Unit, at the Kolkata campus on B. T. Road, does research in pattern recognition, machine learning and related areas.
- **Webel**: the West Bengal Electronics Industry Development Corporation Limited, a state corporation. It manages a Centre of Excellence on Data Science and Machine Learning for the state's Information Technology and Electronics Department, which advertised student internships in February 2025.

## How do I find openings with these employers?

Go to the source. Every employer listed here publishes openings on its own website, and that is the safest place to apply.

1. **Use official careers pages.** Search each employer's careers site and filter by Kolkata. Bookmark the pages linked in the sources below rather than relying on reposts.
2. **Use government job portals.** The [National Career Service](https://www.ncs.gov.in/) of the Ministry of Labour and Employment lists jobs, internships and job fairs, and says its services are free. West Bengal's [Employment Bank](https://www.employmentbankwb.gov.in/) is a state government portal for jobseekers.
3. **Watch research and public bodies.** C-DAC, ISI and Webel post project positions and internships on their own sites, often with short deadlines.
4. **Work with your placement cell.** If you are a student, your college placement office will know which employers recruit on campus.
5. **Build visible evidence.** A small portfolio of projects, a tidy code repository and a clear CV help more than a long list of certificates.

## How do I avoid fake job offers?

Fraudsters often use the names of well-known companies. A few simple checks protect you.

- **Never pay to get a job.** PwC India's Kolkata careers page states that it does not ask for payment at any stage of selection, and the National Career Service tells jobseekers not to pay fees to employers.
- **Check the address.** Offers should come from the employer's own email domain and link to its official careers site.
- **Be wary of instant offers.** A genuine process normally includes an application, tests or interviews, and a written offer.
- **Report suspicious offers.** Use the fraud-reporting contact on the employer's official site.

## Why are start-ups not listed?

Kolkata also has technology start-ups, but their size, location and focus change quickly, and many do not publish an office address. Rather than list some and miss others, this guide leaves them out. Start-up openings appear on the same government portals and on company websites.
