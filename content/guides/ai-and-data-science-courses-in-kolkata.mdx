Kolkata has a long history in statistics and computing, and several public institutions in and around the city now teach AI and Data Science at different levels. This guide lists programmes that we could confirm on each institution's official website on 23 November 2026. It does not rank them, and it leaves out fees and admission dates, which change every year.

## What about computer science degrees with AI content?

Many people reach AI and Data Science through a computer science degree. Jadavpur University also offers a B.E. and an M.E. in Computer Science & Engineering, and its department lists machine learning, natural language processing and computer vision among its research areas. The University of Calcutta's department lists a B.Tech. in Computer Science & Engineering, which, according to its eligibility rules, is entered after a B.Sc. (Hons) in a related subject.

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), whose campus is at Haringhata in Nadia district, lists a B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering, an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering and an M.Tech in Software Engineering. Its department page does not name a separate AI or Data Science programme.

## How do I check whether a course is recognised?

Recognition matters for jobs, government posts and further study. These checks take a few minutes and use only official sources.

1. **Check the university.** The University Grants Commission (UGC) lists recognised universities and publishes a [state-wise list of fake universities](https://www.ugc.gov.in/universitydetails/Fakeuniversity), last updated in February 2026. Make sure the body awarding your degree is not on it.
2. **Check technical approval.** Engineering, technology and computer applications programmes in colleges usually need approval from the All India Council for Technical Education. The [AICTE website](https://www.aicte.gov.in/) links to a dashboard where you can search approved institutes.
3. **Check online or distance degrees.** The [UGC Distance Education Bureau](https://deb.ugc.ac.in/) lists recognised open, distance and online programmes. A university's campus approval does not automatically cover its online degrees.
4. **Look at quality ratings.** The [National Assessment and Accreditation Council](https://naac.gov.in/index.php/en/) (NAAC) grades institutions. Some programmes also carry accreditation from the National Board of Accreditation (NBA): Jadavpur University, for example, states that its undergraduate computer science programme is NBA accredited up to 30 June 2030.
5. **Read the official notice.** Programme names, eligibility, seats and fees should match what the institution itself publishes. If a third-party website says something different, trust the official page.

## How should I judge a private training institute?

Kolkata has many private training centres and online providers offering courses in AI, data science and analytics. We do not list or rate them. Instead, ask each one the same questions and compare the answers in writing.

- **Who awards the certificate?** A certificate from a training company is not a degree. If a provider says its course leads to a degree, ask which university awards it and check that university on the UGC and Distance Education Bureau sites.
- **What exactly will you learn?** Ask for the full syllabus, the number of live teaching hours and the projects you will complete. Compare it with the topics in a university syllabus such as those linked above.
- **Who teaches?** Ask for the names and backgrounds of the instructors, not only of guest speakers.
- **What is promised about jobs?** Treat a "guaranteed job" or a fixed salary promise with caution. Ask for the terms in writing, including what happens if no job follows.
- **What are the full costs and refund terms?** Get every fee, instalment and refund rule in writing before you pay.

Decide first what you need: a full degree, a postgraduate diploma, or a short course to add one skill. Then shortlist two or three options from the table, read each official admission notice in full, and note the entrance test and dates.
