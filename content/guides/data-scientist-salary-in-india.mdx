Salary is one of the most searched questions about data science in India, and one of the hardest to answer honestly. Figures online range widely, are often undated and rarely say how they were collected. This guide sets out what published sources say, how they differ, and what drives pay for an individual.

All figures below are given with their source and date. They describe the past and will go out of date. They are not a promise of what any job will pay.

## Is there an official figure for data scientist pay?

Not that we could find. One of India's main official sources on earnings is the [Periodic Labour Force Survey](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1774607827733_3e8964a9-268b-4cc9-ad65-cfc8a9e32f08_Press_note_AR_PLFS_2025_23032025_V2.1_26032026_final.pdf) (PLFS), run by the National Statistics Office. Its press note for 2025, released on 27 March 2026, reports average earnings in regular wage or salaried work of ₹24,217 for men and ₹18,353 for women [PLACEHOLDER: verify that these PLFS figures are monthly earnings; the press note summary does not state the period].

These are averages across all salaried workers in every occupation, so they cannot tell you what a data scientist earns. They give useful context about pay across the whole workforce, but we found no official series that reports pay for data scientists [PLACEHOLDER: verify whether any official dataset reports earnings for data science or analytics occupations].

## What do published salary studies say?

Two kinds of published source give figures specific to data roles: industry salary studies and recruitment firms' salary guides. They use different groups of people, different job definitions and different years, so their numbers do not line up neatly.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AIM Research salary study](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-insights-analysis/analytics-india-salary-study-2024/) | Data and analytics professionals, all levels | Median ₹15.1 lakh a year | Published 3 August 2024 | Not on the public summary page |
| [PERSOL salary guide](https://www.persolapac.com/articles/2025-salary-guide-for-it-engineering-talent-in-india) | Data scientists, 0 to 3 years | ₹9 to 14 lakh a year | 2025 guide; page undated | No |

| PERSOL salary guide | AI and ML engineers, 0 to 3 years | ₹12.5 to 20 lakh a year | 2025 guide; page undated | No |

The AIM Research figure covers a broader group than data scientists, including analysts and other data roles. Its summary also reports that the 2024 median was about 1% lower than the previous year's. The data are more than two years old at the time of writing.

The PERSOL figures come from a recruitment firm's guide. The public page does not explain whether they are based on placements, surveys or recruiter judgement, so treat them as one firm's view rather than a measured average.

## What do salary websites show?

Several websites publish salary figures for data scientists in India, built mainly from pay that users report themselves or from aggregated job listings. Examples include AmbitionBox, Glassdoor and Naukri. They are listed here only as examples, not in any order and not as recommendations.

These figures can be useful for a rough range and for comparing employers. But the data are not checked, the people who report may not be typical, and job titles are applied loosely. ## Why do the figures disagree?

The sources above do not contradict each other so much as measure different things.

- **Different groups.** Some cover only people titled data scientist; others cover all data and analytics roles.
- **Different years.** Pay changes from year to year, and a figure from 2024 may not describe 2026.
- **Different methods.** Self-reported figures, recruiter estimates and survey medians are not directly comparable.
- **Different measures.** A median, an average and a range each tell you something different. A median is the middle value; an average can be pulled up by a few very high salaries.
- **Loose job titles.** One employer's data scientist does work that another calls analytics or machine learning engineering.

Because of this, the honest answer is a range, and any single "average salary" for data scientists in India should be read with care.

## What affects how much a data scientist is paid?

Pay for an individual depends on many factors. These are the ones published sources and job listings point to most often.

1. **Experience.** In the PERSOL guide, the range for 6 to 10 years' experience is at least double the range for 0 to 3 years at both ends.
2. **The exact role.** Job titles cover different work. In the PERSOL guide, AI and ML engineer ranges sit above data scientist ranges at each experience level.
3. **Sector.** AIM Research reported a wide range within pharma and healthcare alone, from ₹7.8 to 19.8 lakh a year depending on experience.
4. **City.** Pay and living costs vary between cities. AIM Research reported medians of ₹14.7 to 14.8 lakh a year for Chennai, Pune and Hyderabad in 2024.
5. **Type of employer.** Start-ups, product companies, IT services firms, consulting firms, banks and government bodies pay differently and structure pay differently.
6. **Work arrangement.** AIM Research reported the highest median, ₹16.1 lakh a year, for people in hybrid work in 2024.
7. **Skills and evidence of work.** Skills that an employer needs, backed by a record of projects, can help a candidate in salary discussions.
8. **Education.** Some roles, especially in research, ask for a master's degree or doctorate and may pay accordingly.

## How should you read a job offer?

The headline number in an offer is often cost to company (CTC). This can include employer provident fund contributions, gratuity, insurance, joining bonuses and variable pay that depends on performance. Your monthly take-home pay will usually be lower than CTC divided by twelve.

Before comparing offers, ask for a breakdown of fixed pay, variable pay and benefits, and how variable pay is decided. Compare like with like.

## How should you use this information?

Use published figures to set rough expectations and to prepare for salary conversations, not as a target you are owed. Check the date and method of any figure you rely on. For first jobs, focus as much on what you will learn and who you will learn from as on the starting number.
